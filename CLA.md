## How to sign

Open a pull request. A bot will comment with a link to this document and the
exact sentence to reply with. Signing takes one comment and is recorded once —
it applies to every future contribution you make.

If you would rather not sign, you can still help: open issues, review designs,
report defects, and discuss architecture. None of that requires a CLA.
